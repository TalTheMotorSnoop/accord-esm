How to Use This Manual

  1. Pick your car — use the Year and Code dropdowns on the left. The code is your specific model (e.g. CL7 = 2.0 i-VTEC Saloon, CN2 = 2.2 i-CTDi Tourer). If you're not sure, it's on your V5/logbook next to "Type".
  2. Choose Shop Manual or Body Repair — Shop Manual covers everything mechanical and electrical (engine, gearbox, brakes, suspension, wiring, etc). Body Repair covers panel removal, structural dimensions, and bodywork procedures.
  3. Browse or search — expand the system/component tree to find what you need, or type a keyword like "timing chain", "brake pad", or "alternator" into the search box and press Enter. Trouble codes work too — type a code like P0301 straight into the search box.
  4. Click a result — the full procedure loads in this area, with step-by-step instructions, diagrams, torque specs, and wiring schematics.
  5. Zoom into diagrams — click the magnifying glass icon on any wiring diagram or exploded view to open a full-size zoomable version in a new window.
  6. Your data stays with you — notes, job cards, bookmarks and pins are saved in this browser only; nothing is uploaded and there are no accounts. Browsers can clear site data after a long absence, so use Export my data at the bottom of the sidebar to keep a copy or move it to your phone or another computer. On the website, anonymous usage stats (which pages, searches and features are used, plus errors and screen size) are collected to improve the manual — never your notes, job cards, bookmarks or pins. You can switch this off at the bottom of the sidebar.

What's Changed vs. the Originals

New in the V2 update
Finding things
  • New Search looks inside every page — not just titles. Pages whose text mentions your keywords appear in a "more in page contents" section.
  • New Smarter search box — autocompletes from the manual's own vocabulary as you type, catches typos with "did you mean", and translates British English automatically (windscreen→windshield, tyre, bonnet, boot, handbrake, gearbox…).
  • New Both manuals searched at once — Shop Manual searches also check the Body Repair manual (and vice versa), with matches in their own section.
  • New Better results box — pin results to keep them visible, keyboard ↑/↓/Enter, sticky result count, your last-opened result stays marked, and the box is resizable.
  • Improved Tree browsing — a filter box instantly narrows the System/Component tree; selecting a folder splits results into "in that system" and "found elsewhere" with a removable filter chip.
  • New Trouble codes in search — type a code like P0301 into the search box: it decodes what the code means and finds every page covering it on your model.
Working on the car
  • New Job Cards — a workspace per vehicle: name, reg, mileage, a tick-off list of attached procedures, and job notes. Activating a card switches to its model; duplicate a card for the next car.
  • New Split View — two pages side by side: steps on the left, wiring diagram on the right. Open anything into the right pane with ⇥, swap sides with ⇆.
  • New Page Notes — stick your own margin notes on any page; they reappear whenever you return, are listed under "My Notes", and show up in search results.
  • New Common Jobs page — a curated index of Honda's own procedures for the jobs owners do most (oil, brakes, filters, plugs, bulbs, safe jacking…).
  • New Unit Converter — mm↔inches, Nm↔lb·ft, bar↔psi, litres↔quarts, °C↔°F in a floating panel.
Reading procedures
  • New Your keywords highlighted inside pages opened from search, with a counter to step between matches.
  • New In-page glossary — Honda acronyms (ABS, A/T, VSA…) get a dotted underline; click for a plain-English definition. Add your own terms too.
  • New Text size controls — A−/A+ scales procedure pages (diagrams included), remembered between sessions.
  • Improved Diagram zoom window — scroll-wheel zoom, drag to pan, double-click to reset.
Everyday
  • New A seventh theme: Fable — a lamplit-library look in amber, copper and parchment.
  • Improved Cleaner Welcome page — the safety disclaimer now appears as a confirmation on every launch (tick to continue) instead of filling the home page.
  • New Guided tour — a spotlight walkthrough of every control, offered on first launch and rerunnable from the Tutorial button on the Home page.
  • New Continue where you left off — your model, last page and last search are remembered; the Welcome page offers one-click returns.
  • New Backup & Restore — export all your pins, notes, jobs and settings to one file; import it on any machine. Perfect for USB-stick users.
  • Improved Interface polish — Service/Trims/Guides live at the top of the sidebar; the sidebar is resizable; browser tabs show the open procedure; multiple tabs stay in sync; Esc closes panels; the Welcome, Service, Trims and Guides pages all follow your theme.
  • Fix Full features from a plain folder or USB stick — modern browsers silently blocked several features when the ESM was opened directly; everything now works either way.
  • Fix Dozens of stability and correctness fixes throughout — search edge cases, theme glitches, data safety on import, and more.
  • New Anonymous usage stats (website only, v2.2) — the site counts which pages, searches and features get used, plus errors, so the weak spots can be fixed. Nothing you write is sent, and there is an off switch at the bottom of the sidebar.

  • New Both generations (Mk7 and Mk8) are combined into one program. Just change the year and the correct manual loads.
  • New Six interface themes: Modern, Classic, Sakura, Mint, Wildfire, and Aurora. Dark themes include dark content pages for garage use.
  • New Bookmarks, browsing history, back/forward navigation, and recently viewed pages.
  • New Quick DTC lookup — filter search results to diagnostic trouble codes only. (Folded into the main search box in V2.)
  • New Shareable URL links to specific procedures.
  • New Honda acronym glossary built into the sidebar.
  • New Keyboard shortcuts for power users.
  • New Service Interval Dashboard — enter your mileage and car age to see what maintenance is due.
  • New Trim Level Guide — see standard equipment, missing features, and optional extras for every trim level.
  • New Wire Colour Decoder — floating panel with all 19 Honda wire colour abbreviations and swatches.
  • Improved Model code dropdown shows what each code actually means (e.g. "CW3 — 2.2 i-DTEC Tourer").
  • Improved Mobile-friendly layout with touch-sized controls.
  • Improved No installation required. No internet connection needed. Just extract the zip and open HONDAESM.HTML.
