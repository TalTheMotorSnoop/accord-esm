Engine Oil Filter Housing Replacement
Removal
  1. Remove the alternator.
  1. Remove the A/C compressor. Do not disconnect the A/C line from the compressor.
  1. Remove the water pump.
  1. Remove the oil filter housing assembly.
  1. Remove the heat shield.
  1. Remove the oil cooler.
  1. Remove the engine oil filter.
Installation
  1. Install the engine oil filter.
  1. Install the oil cooler.
  1. Install the heat shield.
  1. Install the oil filter housing assembly (A) with new O-rings (B).
  1. Install the water pump.
  1. Install the alternator.
  1. Install the A/C compressor.
