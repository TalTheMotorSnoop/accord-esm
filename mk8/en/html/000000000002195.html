DTC Troubleshooting: U0104
DTC U0104:
 F-CAN Malfunction (ECM-ACC Unit)
NOTE: Before you troubleshoot, record all freeze data and review the general troubleshooting information.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs in the DTCs MENU with the HDS.
Are DTC U0029 and U0104 indicated at the same time?
YES -
Go to troubleshooting for DTC U0029. ■
NO -
Go to Step 3 .
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs in the DTCs MENU with the HDS.
Is DTC U0104 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the gauge control module, the ACC unit, and the ECM.■
  1. Check for communication to the driving support system with the HDS.
Does the HDS communicate with the ACC unit?
YES -
Go to Step 6 .
NO -
Go to the DLC circuit troubleshooting. ■
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0), and wait 2 minutes.
  1. Disconnect the ACC unit 20P connector.
  1. Disconnect ECM connector No. 2 (58P).
  1. Connect ECM connector No. 2 terminal No. 54 to body ground with a jumper wire.
  1. Check for continuity between ACC unit 20P connector terminal No. 10 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Go to Step 11 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the ACC unit and the ECM connector No. 2 terminal No. 54, then go to Step 13 .
  1. Connect ECM connector No. 2 terminal No. 41 to body ground with a jumper wire.
  1. Check for continuity between ACC unit 20P connector terminal No. 20 and body ground.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Substitute a known-good ACC unit, then go to Step 13 and recheck. If DTC U0104 is not indicated, replace the original ACC unit, then go to Step 13 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between the ACC unit and the ECM connector No. 2 terminal No. 41, then go to Step 13 .
  1. Reconnect all connectors.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Reset the ECM with the HDS.
  1. Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC U0104 indicated?
YES -
Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the gauge control module, the ACC unit, and the ECM, then go to Step 1 .
NO -
Troubleshooting is complete. If any other Temporary DTCs or DTCs are indicated, go to the indicated DTC's troubleshooting.■
