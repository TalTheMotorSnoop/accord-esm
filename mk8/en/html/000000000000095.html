EPS Control Unit Removal/Installation
NOTE: The illustrations shows LHD model, RHD model is symmetrical.
  1. Do the battery terminal disconnection procedure.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Passenger's dashboard undercover
    • Passenger's front door sill trim
    • Passenger's kick panel
    • Passenger's under-dash fuse/relay box
  1. Remove the harness clip (E) from the EPS control unit bracket.
  1. Disconnect EPS control unit connector A (2P), connector B (2P), connector C (2P), and connector D (28P).
  1. Remove the bolts (F) from the EPS control unit (G).
  1. Remove the EPS control unit.
  1. Install the EPS control unit in the reverse order of removal.
  1. Do the battery terminal reconnection procedure.
  1. Do the memorizing the torque sensor neutral position.
  1. After installation, start the engine, allow it to idle, and turn the steering wheel from lock-to-lock several times. Check that the EPS indicator does not come on.
