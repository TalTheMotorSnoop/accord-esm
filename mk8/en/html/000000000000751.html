DTC Troubleshooting: B1145
DTC B1145:
 Front Passenger's Power Window Position Information Error
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0) and then back to ON (II).
  1. Open and close the front passenger's power window by using the front passenger's power window switch manually.
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1145 indicated?
YES -
Go to Step 5 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for loose or poor connections.■
  1. Check for DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC B1130 and B1131 indicated?
YES -
Troubleshoot the indicated DTC(s) first.■
NO -
Reset the front passenger's power window, and recheck.
