DTC Troubleshooting: P0713 (28)
DTC P0713:
 Open in ATF Temperature Sensor Circuit
NOTE:
  • Before you troubleshoot, record all freeze data and any on-board snapshot with the HDS, and review General Troubleshooting Information.
  • This code is caused by an electrical circuit problem and cannot be caused by a mechanical problem in the transmission.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Check the ATF Temp Sensor (V) with the HDS in the A/T Data List.
Does the ATF Temp Sensor (V) exceed 4.93 V?
YES -
Go to Step 3 .
NO -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the ATF temperature sensor and the TCM.■
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Disconnect the ATF temperature sensor connector.
  1. Connect ATF temperature sensor connector terminal No. 2 to body ground with a jumper wire.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Check the ATF Temp Sensor (V) with the HDS in the A/T Data List.
Does the ATF Temp Sensor (V) drop to 0.07 V or below?
YES -
Go to Step 8 .
NO -
Go to Step 13 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Remove the jumper wire.
  1. Connect ATF temperature sensor connector terminals No. 1 and No. 2 with a jumper wire.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Check the ATF Temp Sensor (V) with the HDS in the A/T Data List.
Does the ATF Temp Sensor (V) remain 0.07 V or below?
YES -
Replace the ATF temperature sensor, then go to Step 17 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between TCM connector terminal No. 19 and ATF temperature sensor connector terminal No. 1, then go to Step 17 .
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0).
  1. Remove the jumper wire.
  1. Disconnect the TCM connector.
  1. Check for continuity between TCM connector terminal No. 21 and ATF temperature sensor connector terminal No. 2.
Is there continuity?
YES -
Check for poor connections or loose terminals between the ATF temperature sensor and the TCM. If the connections are OK, go to Step 23 .
NO -
Repair open in the wire between TCM connector terminal No. 21 and ATF temperature sensor connector terminal No. 2 , then go to Step 17 .
  1. Reconnect all connectors.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II).
  1. Clear the DTC with the HDS.
  1. Start the engine in P, and wait for at least 20 seconds with the ATF temperature value between −20 °C (−4 °F) and 110 °C (230 °F).
  1. Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC P0713 indicated?
YES -
Check for poor connections or loose terminals between the ATF temperature sensor and the TCM, then go to Step 1 .
NO -
Go to Step 22 .
  1. Monitor the OBD STATUS for P0713 in the DTCs MENU with the HDS.
Does the HDS indicate PASSED?
YES -
Troubleshooting is complete. If any other Temporary DTCs or DTCs were indicated in Step 21 , go to the indicated DTC's troubleshooting.■
NO -
If the HDS indicates FAILED, check for poor connections or loose terminals between the ATF temperature sensor and the TCM, then go to Step 1 . If the HDS indicates NOT COMPLETED, go to Step 20 .
  1. Reconnect all connectors.
  1. Update the TCM if it does not have the latest software, or substitute a known-good TCM.
  1. Start the engine in P, and wait for at least 20 seconds with the ATF temperature value between −20 °C (−4 °F) and 110 °C (230 °F).
  1. Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.
Is DTC P0713 indicated?
YES -
Check for poor connections or loose terminals between the ATF temperature sensor and the TCM. If the TCM was updated, substitute a known-good TCM, then go to Step 25 . If the TCM was substituted, go to Step 1 .
NO -
Go to Step 27 .
  1. Monitor the OBD STATUS for P0713 in the DTCs MENU with the HDS.
Does the HDS indicate PASSED?
YES -
If the TCM was updated, troubleshooting is complete. If the TCM was substituted, replace the original TCM. If any other Temporary DTCs or DTCs were indicated in Step 26 , go to the indicated DTC's troubleshooting.■
NO -
If the HDS indicates FAILED, check for poor connections or loose terminals between the ATF temperature sensor and the TCM. If the TCM was updated, substitute a known-good TCM, then go to Step 25 . If the TCM was substituted, go to Step 1 . If the HDS indicates NOT COMPLETED, go to Step 25 .
