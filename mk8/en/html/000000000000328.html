Middle Floor Undercover Replacement
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Take care not to scratch the body.
  1. Remove the left middle floor undercover (A) and the right middle floor undercover (B).
    -1.
    Remove the bolt (C) that secure the rear strake (D) and the undercover to the body.
    -2.
    Remove the bolts (E) and the nut (F).
    -3.
    Release the hook(s) (G).
  1. Install the undercover in the reverse order of removal. First attach the hooks, and set the undercover between the rear strake and the body, then install the bolts starting at the rear.
