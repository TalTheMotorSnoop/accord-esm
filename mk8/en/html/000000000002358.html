Fuel Sub Strainer Replacement
A/T model
  1. Do the battery terminal disconnection procedure.
  1. Remove the strut brace.
  1. Remove the upper cover (A), then remove the two positive (+) terminals (B) from the under-hood fuse relay box.
  1. Remove the under-hood fuse relay box from the bracket.
  1. Remove the fuel feed hoses (A) from the fuel sub strainer (B).
  1. Remove the harness cramps (C) and the bolts (D), and remove the fuel sub strainer assembly.
  1. Remove the fuel sub strainer (A) from the bracket (B).
  1. Install the parts in the reverse order of removal with new O-rings.
    NOTE: Coat the new O-ring with clean fuel.
  1. Do the battery terminal reconnection procedure.
