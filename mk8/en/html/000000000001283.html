M/T Differential Backlash Inspection
R20A3 Engine Model
  1. Place the differential assembly on V-blocks (A), and install the intermediate shaft (B) and the left driveshaft (C).
  1. Measure the backlash of both pinion gears (D) with a dial indicator (E). When the backlash is not within the standard, check the differential carrier condition, and replace it if necessary.
    Standard (New):
    0.028−0.103 mm (0.0011−0.0041 in.)
