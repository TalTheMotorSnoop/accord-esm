SRS Symptom Troubleshooting - Side airbag cutoff indicator does not come on
Side airbag cutoff indicator does not come on
NOTE:
  • If the SRS indicator also stays on, go to SRS indicator stays on, but no DTCs are stored.
  • Before replacing the SRS unit, check the SRS unit software version with the HDS. If the software version is not the latest, update the SRS unit software and retest.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to LOCK (0), then wait for 10 seconds.
  1. Turn the ignition switch to ON (II), and check that the SRS indicator comes on for about 6 seconds and then goes off.
Does the side airbag cutoff indicator come on?
YES -
Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time.■
NO -
Go to Step 3 .
  1. Connect the HDS to the data link connector (DLC).
  1. Make sure the HDS communicates with the vehicle and the SRS unit. If it does not communicate, troubleshoot the DLC circuit, for R20A3 engine, for K24Z3 engine.
  1. Do the gauge control module self diagnostic function.
Does the side airbag cutoff indicator come on?
YES -
Faulty SRS unit; replace the SRS unit. ■
NO -
Faulty gauge control module; replace the gauge control module. ■
