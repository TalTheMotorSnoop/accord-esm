Torque Converter Housing Selector Control Shaft Oil Seal Replacement
Special Tools Required

Driver handle 15 x 135L
 07749-0010000

Oil seal driver attachment
 07947-ZV00100
  1. Remove the oil seal (A) from the torque converter housing (B).
  1. Install a new oil seal (A) in the torque converter housing to a depth (B) of 0.5−1.5 mm (0.02−0.06 in.) below the torque converter housing surface using the 15 x 135L driver handle and the oil seal driver attachment.
