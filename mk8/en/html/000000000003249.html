Rear Window Wiper Motor Test
  1. Open the tailgate, and remove the tailgate lower trim panel.
  1. Disconnect the 4P connector (A) from the rear window wiper motor (B).
  1. Test the motor by connecting battery power to the rear window wiper motor 4P connector No. 1 terminal and ground to the No. 3 terminal. The motor should run. If the motor does not run or fails to run smoothly, replace the motor.
  1. Connect an analog ohmmeter between the rear window wiper motor 4P connector No. 2 and No. 3 terminals. The ohmmeter should indicate continuity and no continuity alternately while the motor running. If it does not, replace the motor.
