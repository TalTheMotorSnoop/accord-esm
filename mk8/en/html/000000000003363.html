Fuel Fill Door Actuator Replacement
NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Take care not to scratch the body.
  1. Remove these items:
    • Tailgate weatherstrip, as needed
    • Tonneau cover and cargo net, for some models
    • Under floor cover:
      • -
      With temporary repair kit (TRK)
      -
      With compact spare tyre/with normal spare tyre
    • Rear seat cushion
    • Rear seat belt lower anchor, left side
    • Rear seat side bolster, left side
    • Damper maintenance lid, left side
    • Cargo net striker, left side
    • Rear seat belt anchor wire, left side
    • Cargo area side trim panel, left side
  1. Disconnect the fuel fill door actuator connector (A).
  1. Loosen the bolts, then remove the fuel fill door actuator (A) by sliding it upward, and remove the rod (B) from the grommet (C).
  1. Install the fuel fill door actuator in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:
    • Make sure the actuator connector is plugged in properly, and the rod is inserted to the grommet securely.
    • Apply multipurpose grease to the rod (A) where is slides inside the grommet (B).
    • Make sure the fuel fill door opens properly and locks securely.
