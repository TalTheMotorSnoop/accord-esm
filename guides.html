Common Jobs

A curated index of Honda's own service procedures for the jobs owners do most often.
Nothing on this page is new content. Every link below opens the official Honda procedure from this manual — this page just gathers the ones most owners look for into one place. Pick your model and year in the sidebar first: each link runs a search against your selected model, so you always land on the correct pages for your exact car and engine.
Please read before starting any job. The procedures linked on this page come from Honda's factory service manuals, which were written for trained technicians — they assume mechanical experience, workshop judgment, and access to the correct tools. Only attempt a job if you are confident you can complete it safely and have the right tools and parts before you start. Always use appropriate safety equipment, and support a raised vehicle on rated axle stands at Honda's designated points (see Lift and Support Points) — never work under a vehicle held only by a jack. Some of these jobs involve safety-critical systems such as brakes: work on them must be completed correctly, checked, and tested before the vehicle is driven on the road. If you are unsure at any point, stop and consult a qualified mechanic. You proceed at your own risk — the author and contributors accept no responsibility or liability for injury, or for damage to vehicles or property, arising from the use of this page or the procedures it links to.
